Galois mode

In Galois mode this endpoint does not expose local data. Its tables are views that Galois fills by querying a large language model each time HDN Central sends a query; Ontop then answers over the freshly retrieved rows. The schema covers public knowledge only (no patient data) and the mapping is fixed.
